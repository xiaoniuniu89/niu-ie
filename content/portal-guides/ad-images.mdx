Most people scroll past ads in a second. A good ad image makes them stop. You don't need a designer: a phone photo and a free tool are enough to start.

## Sizes that work

Facebook and Instagram show ads in a few shapes. Make each image in the size for where it'll appear:

| Where | Shape | Size in pixels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed (the main scroll) | Square | 1080 × 1080 |
| Feed, taking more of the screen | Portrait (4:5) | 1080 × 1350 |
| Stories and Reels | Tall (9:16) | 1080 × 1920 |

For Stories and Reels, keep text and faces away from the top and bottom of the image, where buttons and your name cover it.

Meta's full list of sizes: [Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide).

## Tools

- **[Canva](https://www.canva.com/):** free, with ready-made templates for every ad size. Search for "Instagram post" or "Facebook ad" to start.
- **Your phone's camera:** real photos of your work, your shop or your team usually do better than stock photos.
- **Canva's background remover** or your phone's photo editor, to cut a product out onto a clean background.

## What makes a good ad image

- **One idea per image.** One product, one offer, one message.
- **Few words.** A short headline at most. Put the details in the text above the image instead.
- **Bright and clear.** Shoot in daylight, near a window or outside.
- **Real people and real work.** A before-and-after, a finished job, a happy customer (ask their permission first).
- **Your brand.** Same colours and logo each time so people start to recognise you.

<Tip>
Make two or three versions and run them side by side. After a few days Meta shows which one people click more. Keep that one and try a new challenger.
</Tip>

## Running your first ad

1. Set up your [Meta business account](/portal/guides/meta-business) first.
2. Go to [Ads Manager](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/) and press **Create**.
3. Pick a goal, like **Traffic** (visits to your website) or **Leads** (people contacting you).
4. Set a daily budget you're comfortable losing while you learn. You can stop the ad at any time.
5. Choose your audience, such as people within a few kilometres of you.
6. Upload your images, write a short message, add your website link, and publish.

Meta reviews each ad before it runs, usually within a day. More help: [Meta Blueprint](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/), Meta's free courses.
