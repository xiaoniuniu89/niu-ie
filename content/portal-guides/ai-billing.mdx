There are two ways to pay for AI. Which one you need depends on **who** is using it.

## Subscription: for people

You pay a fixed amount each month to chat with an AI yourself, in an app or on a website. See [Buy an AI subscription](/portal/guides/ai-subscriptions).

- **Like:** a gym membership. Same price whether you go once or every day.
- **Limits:** if you use it a lot, you may be asked to wait a few hours or upgrade.
- **Good for:** you and your staff writing, planning and researching.

## Pay per token: for your website or software

When your website uses AI itself (for example, a chatbot that answers customers, or a tool that writes product descriptions), it talks to the AI company through an **API**. An API is a way for one piece of software to talk to another. You pay for what it uses.

- **Like:** a phone bill. Each call costs a little, and the total depends on how much you talk.
- **Tokens:** AI companies count text in "tokens", small chunks of words. A short sentence is roughly 10 to 20 tokens. You pay per million tokens, for what goes in and what comes out.
- **Good for:** AI features on your site, used by your customers.

A subscription **doesn't** cover API use. They're separate accounts with separate bills, even at the same company.

## Setting up API billing

If your site needs AI, you'll usually create the account yourself so the bills come to you:

1. Sign up for the developer platform: [Claude Platform](https://platform.claude.com/) (Anthropic) or [OpenAI Platform](https://platform.openai.com/).
2. Add a card and some credit under **Billing**.
3. **Set a monthly spending limit.** This is the most important step. It stops a surprise bill if something goes wrong.
4. Create an **API key**, a long password for your website.
5. Send us the key with [a one-time link](/portal/guides/send-secrets).

<Warning>
An API key is like a credit card number. Anyone who has it can run up charges on your account. Never email it or paste it into a request.
</Warning>

## Which costs more?

It depends on use. A quiet chatbot might cost very little each month. A busy one could cost more than a subscription. The spending limit keeps you in control either way, and the platform's billing page shows what you've used so far.
