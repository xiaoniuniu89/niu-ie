An AI subscription gives you an assistant you can chat with: to write emails and posts, answer questions, or, with the right tools, change your website's code.

## The main options

| Assistant | Made by | Plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Claude](https://claude.ai/) | Anthropic | [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) |
| [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) | OpenAI | [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) |
| [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) | Google | [gemini.google/subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) |

All three have a free version. Try the free one for a week before paying.

## Picking a plan

Each company has roughly three levels:

- **Free:** fine for trying it out. Limited use per day.
- **Personal paid plan:** more use, better models, extra features. The right choice for most small businesses.
- **Team or business plans:** for several people, with shared billing and admin controls. Worth it once more than one or two people use it.

If you want to use AI to edit your website's code yourself, check the plan includes the coding tool. For Claude that's [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code).

## Buying it

1. Sign up on the assistant's website with your **business email**, not a personal one, so the account stays with the business.
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Upgrade** or **Plans**.
3. Pay with a business card, so the receipts go to the business.
4. Download the invoices from the billing page for your accountant.

<Tip>
A subscription is for **people** chatting with AI. If you want an AI feature **on your website**, like a chatbot for customers, that's billed differently. See [Subscription or pay per token?](/portal/guides/ai-billing)
</Tip>
