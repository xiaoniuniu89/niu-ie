A domain is your website's address, like **yourbusiness.ie** or **yourbusiness.com**. You rent it by the year from a company called a **registrar**. You can buy it yourself, or ask Daniel to do it.

## Option 1: Ask Daniel to buy it

The easiest way.

1. Open your project and go to **Feature requests**.
2. Press **Request a feature**.
3. For the title, write something like "Buy a domain".
4. List the names you'd like, best first, in case your first choice is taken.

Daniel buys it, connects it to your site, and bills you for the cost.

## Option 2: Buy it yourself

Good if you want everything in your own name and on your own card.

1. **Pick a name.** Short, easy to spell, easy to say out loud. Avoid hyphens.
2. **Check it's free** on a registrar's website. Type the name into their search box.
3. **Buy it.** Well-known registrars include [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/domains/), [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/) and, for .ie domains, [Blacknight](https://www.blacknight.com/).
4. **Turn on auto-renew** so it doesn't expire by accident. If it expires, your site and email stop working and someone else can buy the name.
5. **Tell Daniel** which registrar you used. He'll either ask you to add some DNS records, or ask you to add him to the account. See [Set and manage DNS records](/portal/guides/dns-records).

<Warning>
Never send your registrar password by email. If Daniel needs to log in, use [a one-time link](/portal/guides/send-secrets), or add him as a user if the registrar allows it.
</Warning>

## .ie or .com?

- **.ie** tells people you're Irish. To register one you need a connection to Ireland, such as an address here. Read more at [weare.ie](https://www.weare.ie/).
- **.com** is recognised everywhere, but good names are harder to find.

Many businesses buy both and send one to the other.

<Tip>
Watch out for letters or emails saying your domain is about to expire and asking you to pay. Some are scams from companies you've never used. Only renew through the registrar you bought from.
</Tip>
