Your website has two kinds of cost: the work to build and change it, and the services it runs on.

## What you pay Niu

- **€64.99** to set up your website.
- **€34.99 an hour** for any work after that.

Niu doesn't charge a monthly fee, and you own your site.

## What you pay other companies

Your site also relies on services from other companies. These are separate from Niu's prices, and what you need depends on your site. Common ones:

| Service | What it's for | How it's usually billed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain | Your address, like yourbusiness.ie | Yearly, to a domain company |
| Hosting | The computers that show your site to visitors | Free or monthly, depending on the site |
| Database | Stores information that changes, like bookings or accounts | Free or monthly |
| Email | Your business inbox | Monthly, per person |
| Payments | Taking cards, for example with Stripe | A cut of each payment |
| AI | Chatbots or AI features on your site | Per use, or monthly |

Prices change, so check each company's pricing page, or ask us what your site uses.

## Why some sites need a server

Think of a simple website as a printed brochure. Everyone who visits sees the same pages. The pages can be made once and handed out from cheap storage around the world. Sites like this are often free or very cheap to host.

Some sites need to **remember things** or **do things** for each visitor. Then they need a server (a computer that runs all the time) and usually a database (where the information is kept). For example:

- **Bookings:** the site has to know which times are already taken.
- **Customer logins and portals:** each person sees their own details, like the portal you're using now.
- **Online shops with stock:** the site has to count what's left.
- **AI features:** each question is sent to an AI company and paid for.

More moving parts means more monthly bills, more to keep secure, and more hours of work.

<Tip>
Before asking for a feature, ask yourself: does the site need to remember something for each visitor? If yes, it will probably add running costs. If not, it's usually a one-off change.
</Tip>

## Working out a budget

1. **Setup:** €64.99, once.
2. **Changes:** the hours we estimate for the work you want, times €34.99.
3. **Running costs:** add up the yearly and monthly bills from the services your site uses. Turn it into one yearly figure: yearly bills plus 12 times the monthly ones.

Not sure how many hours something will take? Send a feature request describing it and ask for an estimate. See [Report an issue or request a change](/portal/guides/requests).
