DNS is the internet's phone book. When someone types your address, DNS tells their computer where to find your website and where to deliver your email. Each entry in the phone book is called a **record**.

You change records where you bought your domain (or where we moved them, such as Cloudflare). Look for a page called **DNS**, **DNS records** or **Manage DNS**.

Want the longer version? Cloudflare has a clear explainer: [What is DNS?](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/dns/what-is-dns/)

## The records you'll meet

| Record | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Points your domain to a server's number (IP address) | `@` points to `76.76.21.21` |
| **CNAME** | Points one name to another name | `www` points to `cname.vercel-dns.com` |
| **MX** | Says where your email is delivered | Your email provider's servers |
| **TXT** | Notes that prove you own the domain, or protect your email | `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all` |

These examples show the shape of each record. Copy the exact values we or the service give you.

Each record has a few boxes:

- **Type:** A, CNAME, MX or TXT.
- **Name** (sometimes **Host**): `@` means the domain itself. `www` means www.yourdomain.ie.
- **Value** (sometimes **Target** or **Points to**): where it points.
- **TTL:** how long computers remember the answer. Leave it on the default or **Auto**.

## Adding a record

1. Log in to your registrar and open your domain's DNS page.
2. Press **Add record**.
3. Pick the type, then copy in the name and value exactly as you were given them.
4. Save.

Changes can take from a few minutes to a day or two to reach everyone. You can see progress on [whatsmydns.net](https://www.whatsmydns.net/).

<Warning>
Don't delete **MX** or **TXT** records unless you're sure what they do. Deleting the wrong one can stop your email arriving. If in doubt, take a screenshot of the whole DNS page before you change anything, and send it to us.
</Warning>

## When to ask us

If a service asks you to "verify your domain" or "add these records" and you're not sure what to do, send a feature request with a screenshot of the instructions. We can tell you what to add, or do it for you if you add us to your registrar account.
