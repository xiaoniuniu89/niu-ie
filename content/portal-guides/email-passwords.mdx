Your website can send email through most email accounts, not only Gmail. For the common ones, this is what we need from you.

Using Gmail? See [Create a Gmail app password](/portal/guides/gmail-app-password).

## Outlook, Hotmail and Live

Personal Microsoft accounts also use **app passwords**.

1. Turn on **two-step verification** at [account.microsoft.com/security](https://account.microsoft.com/security), under **Advanced security options**.
2. On the same page, find **App passwords** and press **Create a new app password**.
3. Copy the password it shows.
4. Send it to us with [a one-time link](/portal/guides/send-secrets).

Microsoft's own guide: [How to get and use app passwords](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/how-to-get-and-use-app-passwords).

<Tip>
Using Microsoft 365 through work? Your administrator may have turned app passwords off. Ask them, or tell us and we'll suggest another way.
</Tip>

## Email from your web host or another provider

If your email comes with your domain or hosting (for example, from Blacknight, GoDaddy or IONOS), we need your **SMTP settings**. SMTP is how a website sends email. Your provider lists these in their help pages or your control panel. Search their help for "SMTP settings". They look like this:

| Setting | Example |
| --- | --- |
| SMTP server (host) | `smtp.yourprovider.com` |
| Port | `465` or `587` |
| Username | Usually your full email address |
| Password | Your email password, or an app password if they offer one |

Send the host, port and username however you like. Send the **password** with [a one-time link](/portal/guides/send-secrets) only.

## Changing your password later

If you change the password on an email account your website uses, the site stops sending email. Send us the new one and we'll update it.
