Your website is made of files of code. **GitHub** is the website where those files are kept. Think of it as Google Drive for code, with a full history of every change ever made.

## Why it matters to you

- **It's the master copy of your site.** Every change we make is saved there, with a note saying what changed and when.
- **Nothing is lost.** If a change goes wrong, the site can go back to any earlier version.
- **It's how you own your code.** Whoever's account holds the code controls it. See [Ways to own and work on your code](/portal/guides/owning-your-code).
- **Your requests land there.** When you report an issue or request a feature in this portal, it becomes a task (GitHub calls it an "issue") on your project.

To see your project on GitHub, pick it under **Projects** in the portal sidebar, then press **Code on GitHub**.

## Create an account

You only need one if you want to see your code, or own it yourself. It's free.

1. Go to [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup).
2. Enter your email, a password and a username. Your username is public, so something like your business name works well.
3. Confirm your email address with the code GitHub sends you.
4. Choose the **Free** plan when asked.
5. Send us your username.

GitHub's own guide: [Creating an account on GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/how-tos/account-management/creating-an-account-on-github).

## Protect your account

Turn on **two-factor authentication**, so logging in needs your password and a code from your phone. GitHub will ask you to do this anyway.

Guide: [Configuring two-factor authentication](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/securing-your-account-with-two-factor-authentication-2fa/configuring-two-factor-authentication).

<Warning>
Save your **recovery codes** somewhere safe, like a password manager or printed in a drawer. If you lose your phone without them, you can be locked out of your account for good.
</Warning>

## Accepting an invite

When we add you to a project, GitHub emails you an invite. Press **View invitation**, then **Accept**. Invites expire after a week, so ask us to resend it if you miss it.
