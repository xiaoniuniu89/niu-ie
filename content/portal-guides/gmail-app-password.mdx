If your website sends email from your Gmail address (for example, contact form messages), it needs an **app password**. This is a separate 16-letter password for your website only. Your normal Gmail password stays private, and you can cancel the app password any time without affecting anything else.

Google's own guide: [Sign in with app passwords](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833).

## Before you start

App passwords only work once **2-Step Verification** is on. That's the setting where Google sends a code to your phone when you log in. If it isn't on yet:

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/security](https://myaccount.google.com/security).
2. Under **How you sign in to Google**, press **2-Step Verification** and follow the steps.

More help: [Turn on 2-Step Verification](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185839).

## Create the app password

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords). Sign in if asked.
2. Type a name you'll recognise, like "Website".
3. Press **Create**.
4. Google shows a 16-letter password in a yellow box. Copy it now. Google won't show it again.
5. Send it to us with [a one-time link](/portal/guides/send-secrets), and tell us which Gmail address it belongs to.

<Tip>
Don't see the app passwords page? It's usually for one of these reasons: 2-Step Verification isn't on, your account uses Google's **Advanced Protection**, or it's a work or school account where the administrator has turned app passwords off. Tell us which one and we'll suggest another way.
</Tip>

## Cancelling it later

Go back to [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords) and press the bin icon next to it. Your website will stop sending email until it gets a new one.

<Warning>
Changing your main Google password cancels all your app passwords. If your site's emails stop after you change your password, create a new app password and send it to us.
</Warning>
