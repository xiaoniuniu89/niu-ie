A **Google Business Profile** is the box that appears on Google Maps and on the right of Google search when someone looks up your business. It shows your hours, phone number, photos, reviews and a link to your website. It's free, and for local businesses it's often how customers find you first.

## Create your profile

1. Go to [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/) and press **Manage now**. Sign in with the Google account you want to own it, ideally a business one.
2. Type your business name. If it appears in the list, someone (or Google) has already created it. Pick it and claim it.
3. Choose your business category, like "Plumber" or "Café".
4. Add your address if customers visit you. If you go to them, add the areas you serve instead.
5. Add your phone number and website address.
6. **Verify** your business. Google may ask for a short video of your premises, a phone call or text, or a postcard with a code. Follow what it offers you.

Google's own guide: [Add or claim your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778).

## Make it useful

- **Hours**, including holiday hours, so nobody turns up to a closed door.
- **Photos:** outside, inside, your work, your team. Real photos beat stock images.
- **Description:** a few plain sentences on what you do and where.
- **Reviews:** ask happy customers to leave one, and reply to every review, good or bad. Replies show new customers you care.

## Give Daniel access

If you'd like Daniel to help manage it, add him as a manager instead of sharing your password:

1. Open your profile on Google (search for your business name while logged in).
2. Go to **Menu** (the three dots), then **Business Profile settings**, then **People and access**.
3. Press **Add**, enter Daniel's email, choose **Manager**, and send.

Guide: [Manage users on your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100).
