Meta owns Facebook and Instagram. A **business portfolio** (Meta used to call it Business Manager) keeps your Facebook page, Instagram account and ads together, separate from your personal profile. You need one to run ads, and to give people like Daniel access without sharing your password.

## Before you start

- A personal Facebook account. Meta requires one to manage a business, but your personal posts stay separate and customers don't see them.
- A **Facebook page** for your business. If you don't have one: on Facebook, open the menu, choose **Pages**, then **Create new Page**.
- An **Instagram** account for your business, if you use Instagram. Switch it to a professional account in the Instagram app under **Settings**, **Account type and tools**.

## Create your business portfolio

1. Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and log in with your Facebook account.
2. Follow the prompts to create a business portfolio: your business name, your name, and your business email.
3. Confirm your email with the link Meta sends.
4. In **Settings**, add your Facebook page and Instagram account to the portfolio.

Meta's help centre: [Meta Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help).

## Give Daniel access

1. In [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/), open **Settings**, then **People**.
2. Press **Invite people**, enter Daniel's email, and choose what he can manage.
3. Meta emails him the invite.

You stay the owner and can remove access any time.

<Warning>
Turn on **two-factor authentication** on your personal Facebook account. Hacked Facebook accounts are common, and whoever gets into yours can take over your business page too. Go to Facebook **Settings**, then **Accounts Centre**, then **Password and security**.
</Warning>
