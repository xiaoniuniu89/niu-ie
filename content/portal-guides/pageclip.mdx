A contact form needs somewhere to send the messages people type in. [Pageclip](https://pageclip.co/) is a service that does exactly that. It collects each message, stores it in a list you can look through or download, and can email you when a new one arrives. Your site doesn't need a server of its own for this.

## Set it up

1. Go to [pageclip.co](https://pageclip.co/) and create an account.
2. Choose a plan. Check their pricing page for what's included.
3. Pageclip gives you a **site key**. It's a long string of letters and numbers.
4. Send the site key to Daniel. He'll connect your form to it.

The site key isn't secret, since it ends up in your website's code, so you can send it by email.

## After it's live

- Log in to Pageclip to see every message, or download them all as a spreadsheet.
- Turn on email notifications in Pageclip's settings so you hear about new messages.
- If messages stop arriving, check your spam folder first, then tell Daniel.

Pageclip's own documentation: [pageclip.co/docs](https://pageclip.co/docs).
