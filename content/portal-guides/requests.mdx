Found something broken, or want something new on your site? Tell us through the portal. Each request goes onto our to-do list, and you can follow it from your project page.

## Where to find it

1. Pick your project under **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Open the **Issues** tab if something is broken, or the **Feature requests** tab if you want something added or changed.
3. Press **Report an issue** or **Request a feature** and fill in the form.

You can edit or cancel a request later from the same tab.

## Reporting an issue

A good report answers three questions:

- **Where is it?** The page, or the address from the top of your browser.
- **What happens now?** What you did, and what went wrong.
- **What should happen?** What you expected instead.

| Hard to fix | Easy to fix |
| --- | --- |
| "The form is broken." | "On the Contact page, I press Send and nothing happens. It should say the message was sent." |
| "The site looks weird on my phone." | "On my iPhone, the menu button on the home page covers the logo." |

<Tip>
A screenshot is often worth more than a paragraph. Add one with **Add screenshots**. On a phone, press the power and volume-up buttons together. On a Mac, press Cmd + Shift + 4. On Windows, press Windows + Shift + S.
</Tip>

Also useful: which phone or computer you were using, and whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

## Requesting a feature or change

Say what you want and why. The "why" helps: we may know a simpler way to get the same result.

- **What would you like?** "A page with photos of our recent jobs."
- **Why would it help?** "Customers keep asking to see examples before they book."

If you've seen something you like on another website, paste the link. Sketches and mockups help too, even a photo of a drawing on paper.

## Asking for things outside the website

Requests aren't only for code. Use **Request a feature** to ask us to buy a domain, set up an email account, or move your code to your own GitHub. Describe what you want in plain words and we'll take it from there.

<Tip>
One request per problem. Three small problems in one request are hard to track. Three requests can each be fixed and closed on their own.
</Tip>
