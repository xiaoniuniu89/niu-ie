[Stripe](https://stripe.com/) lets you take card payments online. A **payment link** is a web address that opens a ready-made checkout page. Put it on a button on your site, in an email, or in a message, and people can pay you without you building a shop.

## 1. Create a Stripe account

1. Go to [dashboard.stripe.com/register](https://dashboard.stripe.com/register) and sign up with your business email.
2. Stripe asks about your business: type, address, what you sell, and your bank account for payouts. Have these ready.
3. Stripe may ask for ID to confirm who you are. This is normal.

Until this is finished you can only use **test mode**, which works with pretend cards.

## 2. Create a payment link

1. In the Stripe dashboard, go to **Payment Links** (search for it in the top bar if you can't see it).
2. Press **New** (or **+ Create payment link**).
3. Add a product: name, price, and a photo if you like. For donations or custom amounts, choose **Customers choose what to pay**.
4. Change any options you want, such as collecting a phone number or allowing a quantity.
5. Press **Create link**, then **Copy**.

Stripe's own guide: [Create a payment link](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links/create).

## 3. Send it to Daniel

Send the link and tell him where on the site it should go ("a Pay deposit button on the Bookings page"). Payment links aren't secret, so email is fine.

<Tip>
Make sure the link is from **live mode**, not test mode. Test-mode links have a "Test mode" badge, and nobody can really pay through them.
</Tip>

## Do you need more than a link?

Payment links are great for a few fixed products or services. If you need a shopping cart, stock counts, or customer accounts, that needs a proper shop on your site. See [What your site costs, and why](/portal/guides/costs).

If Daniel ever asks for Stripe **API keys**, send the secret key (it starts with `sk_live_`) only with [a one-time link](/portal/guides/send-secrets). Never email it.
</Warning>
