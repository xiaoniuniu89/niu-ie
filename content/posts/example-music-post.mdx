There's something about walking into a pub on a Tuesday night and hearing a fiddle, a bodhrán, and a flute locked into a reel together. No stage, no setlist — just musicians sitting in a circle, playing tunes they all know by heart.

## The Session

Irish traditional music sessions are one of the most democratic forms of live music. Anyone can sit in, as long as they can keep up. There's no conductor, no leader — just an unspoken understanding of tempo, dynamics, and when to let someone else take the melody.

## What I've Been Listening To

Lately I've been going back through the Planxty discography. Christy Moore, Andy Irvine, Dónal Lunny, Liam O'Flynn — four musicians who took traditional Irish music and gave it a completely new energy in the 1970s.

Their self-titled debut is still one of the best Irish albums ever made.
