Some of the best music I've ever heard came from video games. Here are a few soundtracks that changed the way I think about composition.

## Celeste

Lena Raine's soundtrack for Celeste is a masterclass in emotional storytelling through music. Each chapter's theme reflects the protagonist's mental state — anxious arpeggios giving way to triumphant swells.

## Outer Wilds

Andrew Prahlow created something extraordinary here. The banjo-driven exploration theme contrasts beautifully with the eerie, haunting pieces that play as you uncover the game's mysteries.

## Disco Elysium

British Sea Power's ambient score perfectly captures the melancholy of Revachol. It's one of the few game soundtracks I regularly listen to outside of the game itself.
